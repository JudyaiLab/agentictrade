How Budget Controls Work

Daily Limit — Resets every day at 00:00 UTC. All API calls by your agents count toward this limit.

Per-Call Limit — Any single API call exceeding this amount is automatically blocked.

Monthly Limit — Resets on the 1st of each month. Acts as a hard cap on total monthly spend.

When any limit is reached, your agents receive a 403 response with the reason. They can resume once the limit resets or you increase it.
